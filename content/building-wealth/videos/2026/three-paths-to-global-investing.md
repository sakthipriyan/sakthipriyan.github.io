For an Indian investor seeking global diversification, the key decision is not only **what** to invest in, but **how** to access international markets. This video explores the three primary paths available: Domestic Mutual Funds, Indian ETFs on foreign indices, and Direct Equity via the Liberalised Remittance Scheme (LRS).
