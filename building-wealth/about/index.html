About

Engineer in Chennai with 17 years of experience building distributed software systems. I’ve been investing since 2016 and use this site to document my reasoning, share what I learn, and build open-source tools around personal finance along the way.

Author

  • Based in Chennai; 17+ years building complex software systems and products
  • Long-term investor since 2016; disciplined, analytical, process-first
  • Portfolio evolved from liquid/ELSS/small-cap to rule-based, global, multi-asset
  • International exposure via LRS (ETFs) and domestic funds (Nasdaq 100, etc.)
  • Interests: personal finance education, evidence-based investing, tool-building

Core Principles

  • Simplicity over complexity
  • Diversify across assets and geographies
  • Automate contributions; rebalance via investment
  • Minimize costs and taxes; avoid noise
  • Write your rules — then follow them

Content

  • Focus: simple, diversified, rule-based long-term wealth building
  • Covers: emergency funds, budgeting, risk management, allocation, rebalancing
  • Includes: costs/taxes awareness, behavior and decision frameworks
  • Formats: blogs, slides, videos, tools — practical and concise

Notes

  • Educational content only — not financial advice
  • Views evolve with evidence; content is refined over time
